About me

Statistics graduate.
Analyst at heart.

I'm Filip Popovic, a statistics graduate from the University of Vienna. I enjoy turning messy data into clear, well-founded answers, and I'm now looking for my first full-time role in data analysis or data science.

Background

I've always been curious about what numbers are really hiding and what you can do with them once you unpack them. Especially when the answer makes someone's life even a little bit easier.

For me the best analysis is one that looks so simple and clean, you would think anyone could have done it. Some only see the results. Others will see the work.

Outside of work, you will find me on a football pitch, at the gym or socialising. That love for football also led to my bachelor's thesis on expected goals models. Staying curious, whether it is about data or anything else, is what life is about.
